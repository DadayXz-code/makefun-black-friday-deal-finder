Bargain Beacon — a Black Friday deal finder with 36 real deals, each found and checked at its store on Black Friday 2026. Every card shows the product, store, sale price, usual price, discount, a link to the product at that store, when the price was checked, and an honest verdict (new low, strong price, or routine sale) with its evidence. Search and filters by category, store, and minimum discount work live, and deals can be sorted by biggest discount, biggest saving, lowest price, or name.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — the board on desktop and on a phone. Hand it in with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`.

Screenshots in `screenshots/` are real renders of this site in headless Chromium: the board (desktop), one deal (desktop), and one deal (phone).
